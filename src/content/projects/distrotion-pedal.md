This is my PCB design for a guitar distortion pedal. As an avid guitar player this project combined one of my hobbies with my course work, and acted as an applied introduction to PCB design.

My goal for this project was to learn more about circuit design and more complex circuit elements, such as operational amplifiers, using capacitors as low and high-pass filters and diode clipping, and develop proficiency with electronic design software, like KiCad. Building off the knowledge I learned from my PHYS 158 course, I self-taught myself concepts related to filtering and amplifying AC signals. Then, I designed my circuit schematic and built my PCB layout in KiCad.

A challenge I faced was deciding which specific components to use, such as evaluating the trade offs between electrolytic and ceramic capacitors. Researching about which components are best for each use case deepened my understanding of basic components like resistors and capacitors.
